APP NAME
CURLYmax

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Watch channel videos, explore playlists, take quizzes and save private notes.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Watch, learn and keep your ideas together with CURLYmax, created by Ashinshana Ishan for the @ashiy_ish YouTube channel.

EXPLORE VIDEOS AND PLAYLISTS
Browse channel videos and playlists, find recent uploads and watch with the embedded YouTube player. If a video cannot play inside the app, open it in YouTube.

LEARN WITH VIDEO QUIZZES
Sign in to take available video-linked multiple-choice quizzes. Get answer feedback, see your score and retry to practise what you have learned. Quizzes are added by the creator and may not be available for every video.

KEEP PRIVATE NOTES
Create, edit and delete notes for individual videos. Your notes and quiz history are linked to your account and sync through the service across supported devices.

CHOOSE HOW YOU USE THE APP
Browse and watch as a guest. Sign in with email or Google to use quizzes and private notes. Opt in to notifications for newly published videos and quizzes, and manage notification preferences in Settings.

PRIVACY AND ACCOUNT CONTROL
Read the privacy policy inside the app. Access account deletion in Settings or email a deletion request to curlymax.dev@gmail.com. Account ownership verification is required for email requests. You can also contact this address for support.

CURLYmax requires an internet connection for videos, sign-in and synchronized features. The bundled privacy policy is available offline. Video availability and embedded playback depend on YouTube and the video publisher. YouTube may display ads in its player.

CURLYmax is a creator companion app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.
