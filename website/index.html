Watch and learn with CURLYmax.

CURLYmax is an Android app for exploring channel videos and playlists, testing what you learn with quizzes, and keeping private notes. Browse videos as a guest. If you choose Google sign-in, we use your Google identity to protect and sync your private notes and quiz history. This site explains our privacy policy and account-deletion options.
