ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION

Delete your CURLYmax account.

This page is provided by Ashinshana Ishan | curlymax for CURLYmax users, including users who no longer have the app installed.

In the app

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete. A recent sign-in is required.
  2. Open Settings → Who am I → Clear all my data.
  3. Read the explanation, type DELETE and confirm.

Deleting the publishing account also removes its owner access. Shared catalog content remains.

Without the app

Email curlymax.dev@gmail.com with the subject CURLYmax account deletion request, preferably from the email associated with your account. State that you want the account and associated private data deleted.

We will verify ownership before processing the request. Do not send your password, sign-in codes or private keys. You do not need to post a public GitHub issue, reinstall the app or create a new account.

Email a deletion request

What is deleted?

Your sign-in account, private notes, quiz attempts/scores/points and in-progress quiz records are removed from active app storage. Local account/cache information is cleared after successful in-app deletion. Public videos, playlists and quizzes and other users’ data remain.

Uninstalling the app does not remove server data. Provider backups and limited technical/security records may remain under provider retention settings or applicable requirements; see the privacy policy.
