PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy at CURLYmax

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · Developer: Ashinshana Ishan | curlymax

Contact: curlymax.dev@gmail.com.

Information we process

  • Account: email address, account ID and authentication information needed for email or Google sign-in. You can browse public videos without an account.
  • Your content: private video notes, quiz selections during an attempt, scores, points and attempt timestamps. These support notes, server grading and quiz history.
  • Public content: YouTube video, playlist, channel and public engagement information used to maintain the catalog.
  • Notifications: Firebase Cloud Messaging processes device registration identifiers to deliver public video and quiz announcements when you opt in. Signed-in accounts can synchronize notification read status across devices.
  • Technical data: our service providers may process connection, device and security logs needed to operate and protect the service.

How information is used and shared

Account data is used to authenticate you, keep private notes and quiz history synchronized, prevent abuse and handle support or deletion requests. If you choose Google sign-in, Supabase Auth receives your Google account identifier, email and basic profile fields supplied by Google for account creation and authentication. CURLYmax does not request access to your Gmail or Google Drive content. We do not sell your private notes or quiz history.

Supabase supplies authentication, database storage and backend services. Google supplies Google sign-in, YouTube playback/public metadata and Firebase Cloud Messaging. YouTube embeds connect to Google/YouTube and may process cookies, device information and playback activity under their own policies. YouTube may show ads in its player; CURLYmax does not run its own advertising system.

See Supabase privacy and Google privacy. The public homepage uses Google Analytics to measure visits. Google may process browser and device information and cookies for this purpose. The privacy and account-deletion pages do not load analytics scripts. This site has no contact form. GitHub Pages hosting may process technical connection logs under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Storage and security

The app uses HTTPS connections. Database permissions restrict notes and quiz history to their account. Limited public catalog information and navigation/settings information are cached on your device. The viewer stores a sign-in session so you can return to your account; Studio’s native owner session is held in memory. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Account information, notes and quiz history remain while your account is active unless you delete them. Deleting a note removes it from active app storage. Account deletion removes the sign-in account, associated private notes, quiz attempts and in-progress quiz records from active app storage. Shared public videos, playlists and quizzes remain.

Provider backups and technical/security logs can persist according to provider retention settings and applicable requirements. If specific records must be retained for security or legal reasons, we will explain the applicable reason when processing your request. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete server data.

Request account and associated-data deletion.

Your choices

You can browse as a guest, edit/delete private notes, turn notifications off in the app or Android settings, and request account deletion. Send access, correction or privacy questions to our support email. Do not include passwords or verification codes.

Changes

Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Contact us if you have questions before using account features.
